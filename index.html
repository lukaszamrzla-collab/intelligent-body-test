The Intelligent Body
200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training

Yoga is more than movement. It is a practice that integrates the body, the breath and the mind. The Intelligent Body was created for those who want to teach yoga with depth, confidence and understanding. It blends modern anatomy and biomechanics with the timeless wisdom of yoga, offering a practical and thoughtful approach to both personal practice and teaching. Throughout the training, you will learn how the body moves, how to build a safe and sustainable asana practice, and how to adapt postures to meet the needs of different individuals.

Alongside this, you will explore the essential practices of pranayama, meditation and yoga philosophy, discovering how breath, awareness and movement work together to create a meaningful yoga experience.

This training is designed to help you think like a teacher rather than simply follow sequences. You will learn to observe, ask questions, make informed decisions and guide students with clarity and compassion.

The 90-Minute Protocol

The Journey

01
Minute 0 - 15

The Prelude: Blue Lotus Ceremony

We begin with the ceremonial brewing of the Egyptian Blue Lotus flower. Historically revered by Pharaohs for its subtle psychoactive ability to induce deep relaxation and heightened states of consciousness, this rare botanical acts as a catalyst. As you sip the elixir, you are guided through initial breathwork (Pranayama) to down-regulate the nervous system, transitioning you from the demands of your day into a state of profound receptivity.

02
Minute 15 - 75

The Ascent: Bespoke Asana Protocol

With the mind primed, we enter a 60-minute movement sequence designed exclusively for your anatomical realities. Drawing upon master-level alignment principles and biomechanical intelligence, this protocol physically unwinds the holding patterns of stress. It builds internal heat, expands joint mobility, and fortifies physical resilience. The pace is entirely dictated by your unique needs on that specific day.

03
Minute 75 - 90

The Return: Elevated Savasana & Stillness

The ritual concludes with 15 minutes of guided stillness. As your physical body rests in final relaxation, the profound benefits of the Blue Lotus and the somatic release of the Asana coalesce. You will experience a deeply regenerative state of conscious rest, resetting the parasympathetic nervous system and leaving you equipped with unparalleled mental clarity for the days ahead.
