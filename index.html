The Intelligent Body
200 Hour Yoga Teacher Training

Yoga is more than movement. It is a practice that integrates the body, the breath and the mind. The Intelligent Body was created for those who want to teach yoga with depth, confidence and understanding. It blends modern anatomy and biomechanics with the timeless wisdom of yoga, offering a practical and thoughtful approach to both personal practice and teaching. Throughout the training, you will learn how the body moves, how to build a safe and sustainable asana practice, and how to adapt postures to meet the needs of different individuals.

Alongside this, you will explore the essential practices of pranayama, meditation and yoga philosophy, discovering how breath, awareness and movement work together to create a meaningful yoga experience.

This training is designed to help you think like a teacher rather than simply follow sequences. You will learn to observe, ask questions, make informed decisions and guide students with clarity and compassion.

About The Intelligent Body
And Myself

I discovered yoga 15 years ago, and it was a revelation. It began as a way to build physical strength, but it quickly became the practice that nurtured my mental clarity and redefined my resilience. That transformation was so profound that I knew I had to dedicate my life to sharing it.

Now, as a Yoga Alliance ERYT-500 certified instructor with extensive experience of more than 2200 registered teaching hours and leading yoga teacher training in Abu Dhabi, my mission is to help you unlock the transformative power of your own body.

I hold an MA in education and pedagogy, giving me a deep understanding of how people learn. This background shapes the way I teach – not just by demonstrating poses, but by empowering you with the knowledge and tools you need to build a sustainable, lifelong practice. I believe in a methodical, intelligent approach to yoga, emphasizing proper alignment, safe progression, and the deep connection between breath and movement.

My teaching philosophy is simple: Yoga is for everyone, but your practice must be yours. My approach is rooted in compassion, clarity, and an unwavering commitment to your growth, ensuring that every session is not just a physical exercise, but an educational journey.
